# Transfer rules explorer

- `transfer_rules` with no arguments: every card, how many programmes it reaches and its best rate.
- `transfer_rules` with `cardId`: every airline and hotel partner with ratio, cap and time, the card's daily, monthly and annual limits and fees, and indirect routes that can beat a direct transfer.
- `transfer_rules` with `programmeId`: which cards reach that programme, best rate first.

Quote the date the rules were checked. Rules come from public sources and are not matched to bank portals, so tell the person to verify on the bank site before transferring.
